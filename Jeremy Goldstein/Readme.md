# Dashboard Confessional: Revealing the Secrets of Data Visualization
Minuteman Dashboards constructed using [Google's Looker Studio](https://lookerstudio.google.com/).\
Many are maintained through automated Python scripts that are posted to our [Github Repository](https://github.com/Minuteman-Library-Network/Scripts) such as our [Circulation Dashboard](https://github.com/Minuteman-Library-Network/Scripts/tree/main/Circulation%20Dashboard), [World Language](https://github.com/Minuteman-Library-Network/Scripts/tree/main/World%20Language%20Dashboard), and [Patron Maps](https://github.com/Minuteman-Library-Network/Scripts/tree/main/Patron%20Maps) dashboards.

More information on developing and using scripts such as these may be found in the main conference presentation, [Automating Reports with Python 2: Getting Modular](https://iug2026.sched.com/event/2HZaK/automating-reports-with-python-2-getting-modular)
* Tuesday April 14th at 10:30 CDT in Kansas City
* This presentation has its own [dedicated repository](https://github.com/jmgold/IUG2026--Automating-Reports-with-Python-2/tree/main)

Other Dashboards work with monthly data pulled from Decision Center and cleaned up slightly in Excel's Get and Transform/Power Query feature.  For more on that check out [Julie Cole's excellent two part presentation from the 2021 and 2022 IUG conferences](https://forum.innovativeusers.org/t/get-transform-part-i-ii/506).

Dashboards are limited to users with Minuteman accounts but you may reach out to Jeremy if you are intersted in learning moare about what we have been doing.
